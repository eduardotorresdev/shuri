# Space Grotesk

The three `.woff2` files beside this notice are subsets (latin, latin-ext, vietnamese) of the
**Space Grotesk** variable font — weight axis 300–700 — by Florian Karsten, as served by Google
Fonts.

Licensed under the **SIL Open Font License 1.1**: <https://openfontlicense.org>.
Source: <https://github.com/floriankarsten/space-grotesk>.

They are bundled rather than linked from `fonts.googleapis.com` because the admin is served by the
app itself: an admin behind a firewall, or offline, must not fall back to a system font halfway
through its own design.
